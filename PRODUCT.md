# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who open sutton.top to find the current demos. They are scanning, not reading a biography.

## Product Purpose

A one-page demo index for sutton.top. Success is: land, see the list, click through to a demo (today: AI Portal at portal.sutton.top).

## Brand Personality

Quiet, typographic, specific. The page should feel like a private index, not a product launch.

## Anti-references

- Marketing SaaS landings (hero, KPI row, card grid, marquee)
- The previous large-type system-ui list
- A copied Benji Taylor biography block
- Cream/sand “editorial” templates that are not this reference

## Design Principles

- The list is the page.
- Borrow structure and motion from benji.org’s Writing list, not its intro copy.
- Keep chrome invisible: no nav, no footer clock, no status theater.
- One real link; do not invent demos to fill the layout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA for text contrast. Keyboard focus visible. `prefers-reduced-motion` skips entrance motion and keeps hover opacity changes instant.
